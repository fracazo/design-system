# Copy

Load when: any user-facing string, accessible name, error, empty state,
label, or commit message.
Canonical owner: each product's CLAUDE.md language and copy sections;
`DESIGN.md`, Reject list (voice).

- English per product (rule/english-per-product). Check the product before
  writing a word.
- Short sentences, one idea each. Read aloud; if the rhythm is wrong,
  rewrite (rule/voice-bans).
- Sentence case everywhere except the mono kicker (rule/sentence-case).
- No em dashes (rule/no-em-dash).
- Warm and direct, never clinical, never patronising. The voice is a
  midwife who respects the reader.
- Plain-language medical terms with the clinical term in parentheses where
  it helps ("nitrous oxide (gas and air)"). Terminology lists live in each
  product's CLAUDE.md.
- Claims name only what ships (rule/claim-only-what-ships).
- "Birth plan" for search, "birth preferences" in clinical and community
  copy; both coexist by design.
- Buttons name the action and the object where the consequence matters
  ("Delete plan", not "Confirm"). Loading states keep the label stable.
- Errors say what happened and what to do next, keep the reader's input,
  and never blame.
